# Katana

Katana is a type of Japanese sword primarily used for combat on foot. Katanas cannot be dual-wielded; if held in the offhand while also held in the main hand, the offhand one will be dropped.

## Sheathing & Drawing

When holding a sheath<ItemImage id="tsuki:sheath" scale="0.6"/> in your offhand, press or use [<KeyBind id="key.tsuki.sheath_in" />] to place the katana in the sheath and turn it into a sheathed katana<ItemImage id="tsuki:katana_sheath" scale="0.6"/><ItemImage id="tsuki:sakura_katana_sheath" scale="0.6"/>. When holding a sheathed katana in your offhand with an empty main hand, press or use [<KeyBind id="key.tsuki.sheath_in" />] to draw the katana into your main hand and release a sweeping attack. Attack damage is affected by enchantments.

Sheathed katanas inherit the enchantments of their contained katanas, but cannot be enchanted at an enchanting table, only at a grindstone.
