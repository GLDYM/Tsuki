# Crops & Forestry

## Crops

All crop seeds can be obtained by breaking grass. The [Japanese Farmer](../villager/villager_index.md) sells some seeds and buys various crops.

- Onion<item id="tsuki:onion_seeds"/><item id="tsuki:onion"/>, Radish<item id="tsuki:radish_seeds"/><item id="tsuki:radish"/>, Cabbage<item id="tsuki:cabbage_seeds"/><item id="tsuki:cabbage"/>: Standard crops.
- Canola<item id="tsuki:rapeseeds"/>, Red Bean<item id="tsuki:red_bean"/>, Soybean<item id="tsuki:soybean"/>, Buckwheat<item id="tsuki:buckwheat"/>, Taro<item id="tsuki:taro"/>: Standard crops.
- Tomato<item id="tsuki:tomato_seeds"/><item id="tsuki:tomato"/>, Eggplant<item id="tsuki:eggplant_seeds"/><item id="tsuki:eggplant"/>: Two-block-high crops that continue growing upward after maturity.
- Rice<item id="tsuki:rice_seeds"/>: Must be planted in single-block-deep water. When mature it becomes two blocks high and emerges above water; harvest only the top half to leave the root intact.
- Vanilla<item id="tsuki:vanilla_seeds"/><item id="tsuki:vanilla"/>: Seeds can be found wild. Must be planted on a Vanilla Splint<item id="tsuki:vanilla_splint"/>; harvest with right-click.
- Pepper<item id="tsuki:pepper_seeds"/><item id="tsuki:peppercorn_green"/><item id="tsuki:peppercorn_red"/>: Seeds can be found wild. Must be planted on a Pepper Splint<item id="tsuki:pepper_splint"/>; harvest with right-click. Last immature stage yields Green Peppercorn<item id="tsuki:peppercorn_green"/>, mature yields Red Peppercorn<item id="tsuki:peppercorn_red"/>.
- Hop<item id="tsuki:hop"/>: Must be planted on a vertical Grape Splint Stand<item id="tsuki:grape_splint_stand"/>; harvest with right-click.
- [Grape](./grape.md)<item id="tsuki:grape_seeds"/><item id="tsuki:grape_green"/><item id="tsuki:grape"/>: Detailed planting layout, growth stages, harvesting, and products.
- Edodes<item id="tsuki:edodes"/>，Shimeji<item id="tsuki:shimeji"/>：Put Fallen Maple Leaves on a Maple Log and use bone meal to change them into Mushroom Fallen Leaves<item id="tsuki:fallen_leaves_mushroom"/>. Use shears and right-click Mushroom Fallen Leaves<item id="tsuki:fallen_leaves_mushroom"/> to harvest.
- Matsutake<item id="tsuki:matsutake"/>：Sometimes coverting Mushroom Fallen Leaves can get Matsutake Fallen Leaves<item id="tsuki:fallen_leaves_matsutake"/>. Use shears and right-click Matsutake Fallen Leaves<item id="tsuki:fallen_leaves_matsutake"/> to harvest.

## Saplings & Flowers

- Bamboo Shoot<item id="tsuki:bamboo_shoot"/>: Naturally generates in plains, forests, jungle, swamps, and meadows; can also be obtained by fishing. Grows into Bamboo<item id="tsuki:bamboo"/>. Bonemealing bamboo may spawn Bamboo Shoots within an 1-block radius.
- Sakura Sapling<item id="tsuki:sakura_sapling"/>: Obtained via crafting. A high-yield building material.
- Maple Saplings<item id="tsuki:maple_sapling_red"/><item id="tsuki:maple_sapling_yellow"/><item id="tsuki:maple_sapling_orange"/><item id="tsuki:maple_sapling_green"/>: Obtained via crafting and naturally generate in maple forests. Maple trees always generate fallen leaves<item id="tsuki:fallen_leaves_red"/><item id="tsuki:fallen_leaves_yellow"/><item id="tsuki:fallen_leaves_orange"/><item id="tsuki:fallen_leaves_green"/>. Sometimes a Maple Sap Log<item id="tsuki:maple_sap_log"/> is generated; use [Maple Spile](../cooking/maple_spile.md)<item id="tsuki:maple_spile"/> and [Maple Cauldron](../cooking/maple_spile.md)<item id="tsuki:maple_cauldron"/> to collect maple syrup. Chestnut Burrs<item id="tsuki:chestnut_burrs"/> may occasionally spawn under green maples; right-click to harvest. Opening a Chestnut Burr yields Chestnut<item id="tsuki:chestnut"/>.
- Ume Sapling<item id="tsuki:ume_sapling"/>: Obtained via crafting. Ume leaves occasionally grow Plums<item id="tsuki:ume"/>, which can be harvested with right-click.
- Sunflower<item id="tsuki:sunflower_seeds"/>:  Obtained by breaking grass, could be planted on the dirt type block. The sunflower needs 3-block space to grow and could gain glowstone when harvesting.

## Agricultural Equipment

- [Sprinkler](sprinkler.md)<item id="tsuki:iron_sprinkler"/><item id="tsuki:gold_sprinkler"/><item id="tsuki:diamond_sprinkler"/><item id="tsuki:sakura_diamond_sprinkler"/>: Irrigates farmland and accelerates crop growth; see the detailed guide.

## Fish

- Shrimp<item id="tsuki:shrimp"/>, Bonito<item id="tsuki:bonito"/>: Obtained by fishing. The [Fisherman](../villager/villager_index.md) also sells Shrimp or Bonito.
