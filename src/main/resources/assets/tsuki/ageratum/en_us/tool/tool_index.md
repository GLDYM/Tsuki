# Tools & Weapons

## Tools

- Sakura Diamond Tools<item id="tsuki:sakura_pickaxe"/><item id="tsuki:sakura_axe"/><item id="tsuki:sakura_shovel"/><item id="tsuki:sakura_hoe"/>: Higher durability and better performance compared to netherite equipment.
- Hammers<item id="tsuki:stone_hammer"/><item id="tsuki:iron_hammer"/><item id="tsuki:steel_hammer"/><item id="tsuki:sakura_hammer"/>: Used for forging katanas, and for crafting and breaking [tatara](../smithing/smithing_index.md).
- Fish Knives<item id="tsuki:knife_fish"/><item id="tsuki:sakura_knife_fish"/> / Noodle Knives<item id="tsuki:knife_noodle"/><item id="tsuki:sakura_knife_noodle"/>: Used for processing corresponding foods on the chopping board.
- Broom<item id="tsuki:broom"/>: Right-click fallen leaves to quickly break them; right-click dirt blocks to convert them to grass paths.
- [????](mythic_pickaxe.md)<item id="tsuki:mythic_pickaxe"/>: Forged using paper as a medium to bridge two magically-imbued materials. See detailed page.
- [Magatama](./magatama.md)<item id="tsuki:magatama_white"/><item id="tsuki:magatama_blue"/><item id="tsuki:magatama_green"/><item id="tsuki:magatama_orange"/><item id="tsuki:magatama_pink"/><item id="tsuki:magatama_purple"/><item id="tsuki:magatama_red"/>: Powerful relics unexpectedly uncovered while exploring the past. See detailed page.
- [Yata no Kagami](./yata_no_kagami.md)<item id="tsuki:yata_no_kagami"/>: A soulbound charm that enables noclip while creative flying.

## Weapons

- Shinai<item id="tsuki:shinai"/>: Practice bamboo sword requiring two-handed grip. Right-click to consume durability and release a sweeping attack.
- [Katana](./katana.md)<item id="tsuki:katana"/><item id="tsuki:sakura_katana"/>: See detailed page.
- Kodachi<item id="tsuki:kodachi"/><item id="tsuki:sakura_kodachi"/>: Lower material cost and damage compared to katana; cannot be dual-wielded or placed in sheaths.
- Tachi<item id="tsuki:tachi"/>: Higher material cost and damage compared to katana; cannot be dual-wielded or placed in sheaths.
