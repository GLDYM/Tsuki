# Tskimi Seiran's Mythery Mix

> A bottle of oddly purple liquid.
>
> One sip brings together sweet peach, the bite of vodka, and the scent of roasted rabbit. Hmm... it actually tastes pretty good...
>
> There is a small chance it contains a little Tskimi Seiran bathwater.

If you mix ingredients in the wrong combination, you'll get Tskimi Seiran's Mythery Mix<ItemImage id="tsuki:mythery_mix" scale="0.6"/>.

Aside from nausea, Mythery Mix grants a 7s special effect: each second deals damage equal to 10% of your maximum health.

Drinking Mythery Mix is not recommended; perhaps you should consider other uses...
