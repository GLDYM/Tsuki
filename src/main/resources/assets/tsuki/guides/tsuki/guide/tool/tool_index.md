# Tools & Weapons

## Tools

- Sakura Diamond Tools<ItemImage id="tsuki:sakura_pickaxe" scale="0.6"/><ItemImage id="tsuki:sakura_axe" scale="0.6"/><ItemImage id="tsuki:sakura_shovel" scale="0.6"/><ItemImage id="tsuki:sakura_hoe" scale="0.6"/>: Higher durability and better performance compared to netherite equipment.
- Hammers<ItemImage id="tsuki:stone_hammer" scale="0.6"/><ItemImage id="tsuki:iron_hammer" scale="0.6"/><ItemImage id="tsuki:steel_hammer" scale="0.6"/><ItemImage id="tsuki:sakura_hammer" scale="0.6"/>: Used for forging katanas, and for crafting and breaking [tatara](../smithing/smithing_index.md).
- Fish Knives<ItemImage id="tsuki:knife_fish" scale="0.6"/><ItemImage id="tsuki:sakura_knife_fish" scale="0.6"/> / Noodle Knives<ItemImage id="tsuki:knife_noodle" scale="0.6"/><ItemImage id="tsuki:sakura_knife_noodle" scale="0.6"/>: Used for processing corresponding foods on the chopping board.
- Broom<ItemImage id="tsuki:broom" scale="0.6"/>: Right-click fallen leaves to quickly break them; right-click dirt blocks to convert them to grass paths.
- [????](mythic_pickaxe.md)<ItemImage id="tsuki:mythic_pickaxe" scale="0.6"/>: Forged using paper as a medium to bridge two magically-imbued materials. See detailed page.

## Weapons

- Shinai<ItemImage id="tsuki:shinai" scale="0.6"/>: Practice bamboo sword requiring two-handed grip. Right-click to consume durability and release a sweeping attack.
- [Katana](./katana.md)<ItemImage id="tsuki:katana" scale="0.6"/><ItemImage id="tsuki:sakura_katana" scale="0.6"/>: See detailed page.
- Kodachi<ItemImage id="tsuki:kodachi" scale="0.6"/><ItemImage id="tsuki:sakura_kodachi" scale="0.6"/>: Lower material cost and damage compared to katana; cannot be dual-wielded or placed in sheaths.
- Tachi<ItemImage id="tsuki:tachi" scale="0.6"/>: Higher material cost and damage compared to katana; cannot be dual-wielded or placed in sheaths.
