# Villager Trades

Japanese villagers use a different currency than vanilla villagers—they use Sakura Coins<ItemImage id="tsuki:sakura_coin" scale="0.6"/>. Sakura coins can be obtained by trading with Japanese villagers or by defeating Samurai Illager. Some items can only be obtained through trading.

## Japanese Farmer

The Japanese Farmer works at a Stone Mortar<ItemImage id="tsuki:stone_mortar" scale="0.6"/> and unlocks 4 new trades upon each level-up, with at least one being a buying trade. Tea leaves and cocktail ingredients must be obtained through trading with the Japanese Farmer.

## Samurai Illager

Samurai Illager are swordsmen in Japanese robes wielding tachi or katana with significantly higher attack damage and speed compared to the Vindicator. They appear in bamboo forests or during raids, and defeating them drops Sakura coins.
