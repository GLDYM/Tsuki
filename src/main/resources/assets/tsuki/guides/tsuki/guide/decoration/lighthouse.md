# Lighthouse Illumination

## Usage

Right-click Lighthouse Illumination with an empty hand to open its settings screen. Right-click while sneaking to toggle its enabled state.

When enabled, the lamp rotates and emits two beams through its lens, pointing in opposite directions. The beams do not provide any light level.

## Beam Settings

The settings screen provides the following options:

- Color: Sets the beam color using the color palette on the right side of the screen.
- Length: Sets the beam extension distance, from 1 to 31.
- Width: Sets the beam width, from 1 to 15.
- Transparency: Sets how transparent the beam is.
- Polygon Count: Sets the number of sides in the beam's cross-section, from 2 to 10. A count of 2 produces a flat triangular beam; counts from 3 to 10 produce conical beams.
