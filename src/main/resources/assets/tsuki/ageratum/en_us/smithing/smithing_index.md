# Ore & Smelting

## Iron Sand<item id="tsuki:iron_sand"/>

Iron Sand naturally generates in beach and river biomes. It can be directly smelted into iron ingots, or crafted with bamboo charcoal into a Tatara for higher output.

## Sakura Diamond<item id="tsuki:sakura_diamond"/>

Sakura Diamond Ore<item id="tsuki:sakura_diamond_ore"/><item id="tsuki:deepslate_sakura_diamond_ore"/> naturally generates in small amounts underground in cherry blossom forests, bamboo groves, and [maple forests](../compat/compat_index.md). Breaking it drops Sakura Diamonds, and the drop is affected by Fortune.

Sakura Diamonds can also be obtained by trading with Japanese villagers, and can be sold to Japanese villagers as well.

## Tatara Forging<item id="tsuki:tatara"/>

The Tatara can be crafted using raw iron, iron ore, or iron sand together with bamboo charcoal blocks and a forging hammer:

<recipe id="tsuki:tatara"/>

After placing the Tatara, right-click it with flint and steel or a fire charge to activate it. When an active Tatara receives a random tick, its forging progress increases by 1, and any adjacent inactive Tatara blocks are activated. When forging progress reaches 3, forging is complete. At that point, breaking the Tatara with a pickaxe or forging hammer yields 0-9 iron ingots<item id="minecraft:iron_ingot"/> **or** 0-2 Tamahagane<item id="tsuki:tamahagane"/>.

Compared with direct smelting, the Tatara can significantly increase iron ingot output, and it is also the only way to obtain Tamahagane.
