# Mod History

## Past and Present

In the 1.7.10 era, Bamboo, Maple Tree, and WA held an important place in the modpack ecosystem. Rooted in Japanese traditional culture, they formed the prototype of today's decorative, immersive agriculture mods. At the time, nearly every casual modpack included them.

But the next LTS version came far too late. More than three years passed between 1.7.10 and 1.12.2, and making 1.12.2 stable took nearly another year. That long gap drained creators' enthusiasm, and Japanese-style mods gradually faded.

In 2019, Sakura was released, integrating and refining those three mods while adding a large amount of new content. Sakura's release sparked a revival of Japanese-style mods and saw use in many modpacks.

That momentum did not last for a long time. After 2020, Sakura's update pace slowed significantly, and from 2021 to 2025 it even did not release any stable version at all. In early 2025, Sakura released a 1.20.1 version. But during 2020-2025, Farmer's Delight and Kaleidoscope were released in succession, and with their excellent art and immersive gameplay they occupied much of the agriculture mod space; Urushi, which focuses on Japanese-style decoration and folklore, also entered the scene. Sakura, however, had only migrated a very small portion of its content and still contained many bugs, leaving from the modpacks.

Back in the 1.7.10 era, I encountered these Japanese-style mods through modpacks made by various content creators. By 1.12.2 I was assembling modpacks myself, and by 1.20.1 I was publishing modpacks. In almost every modded playthrough, I added these Japanese-style mods. But I also clearly felt that they were increasingly unable to keep up with the modern mod ecosystem. They had almost no compatibility with other mods, their art style was still stuck in the 1.7.10 era, and their mechanics were often unsatisfactory. If refactoring some simple utility mods to fit the mod ecosystem can work very well, then perhaps large mods can be refactored too?

That is Tsuki. I do not know what the next five years will look like, and I do not know how long I will be able to maintain this mod; but if even one person in the future tries to remake these Japanese-style mods, then this attempt will have meaning.
