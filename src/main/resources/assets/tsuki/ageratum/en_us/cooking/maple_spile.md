# Maple Spile and Maple Cauldron

## Activation

The Maple Spile<item id="tsuki:maple_spile"/> must be placed on the side of a Maple Sap Log<item id="tsuki:maple_sap_log"/>, with a Maple Cauldron directly below it.

The Maple Cauldron<item id="tsuki:maple_cauldron"/> requires a heat source within two blocks below it to function, such as a Magma Block, Campfire, Lava, or a Stove from Farmer's Delight. The space between the cauldron and the heat source must be air or a block that does not block heat, such as a Hopper.

## Usage

Once set up, the Maple Cauldron will continuously collect maple syrup. If a heat source is provided, it will boil the syrup down into item form. You can extract the result with a Hopper.

Harvesting from a Maple Sap Log has a chance to deactivate it. Once deactivated, it turns into a normal Maple Log and no longer produces maple syrup.
