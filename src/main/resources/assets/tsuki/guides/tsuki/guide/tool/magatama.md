# Magatama

Magatama cannot be crafted, and can only be obtained through specific advancements. All magatama are soulbound by default and will not drop on death.

## Effects

- White Magatama<ItemImage id="tsuki:magatama_white" scale="0.6"/>: Negates fall damage and kinetic damage while granting flight, but greatly reduces the holder's max health. Use [<KeyBind id="key.tsuki.magatama_mode_toggle"/>] to switch between Creative Flight, Creative Flight (Instant Stop), and Elytra Flight. In Elytra mode, holding forward launches fireworks without consuming them.
- Blue Magatama<ItemImage id="tsuki:magatama_blue" scale="0.6"/>: Converts the weather at the cost of health. Use [<KeyBind id="key.tsuki.magatama_mode_toggle"/>] to switch the target weather. Cooldown: 60s.
- Green Magatama<ItemImage id="tsuki:magatama_green" scale="0.6"/>: Consumes all hunger, all saturation, and most of your health, then grants 32 seeds or crops. Cooldown: 300s.
- Orange Magatama<ItemImage id="tsuki:magatama_orange" scale="0.6"/>: Lets you keep eating even when full, and continuously converts saturation or hunger into healing. Overflow healing becomes absorption.
- Pink Magatama<ItemImage id="tsuki:magatama_pink" scale="0.6"/>: Automatically restores hunger and saturation every 60s. Using it restores them instantly and starts a 60s cooldown.
- Purple Magatama<ItemImage id="tsuki:magatama_purple" scale="0.6"/>: Clears nearby monster aggro. After you attack, it enters a 30s cooldown during which you take double damage.
- Red Magatama<ItemImage id="tsuki:magatama_red" scale="0.6"/>: Every 2s executes specific entities within 32 blocks. Using it executes them instantly.
