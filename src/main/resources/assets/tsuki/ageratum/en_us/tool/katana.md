# Katana

Katana is a type of Japanese sword primarily used for combat on foot. Katanas cannot be dual-wielded; if held in the offhand while also held in the main hand, the offhand one will be dropped.

## Sheathing & Drawing

When holding a sheath<item id="tsuki:sheath"/> in your offhand, press or use [<key id="key.tsuki.sheath_in"/>] to place the katana in the sheath and turn it into a sheathed katana<item id="tsuki:katana_sheath"/><item id="tsuki:sakura_katana_sheath"/>. When holding a sheathed katana in your offhand with an empty main hand, press or use [<key id="key.tsuki.sheath_in"/>] to draw the katana into your main hand and release a sweeping attack. Attack damage is affected by enchantments.

Sheathed katanas inherit the enchantments of their contained katanas, but cannot be enchanted at an enchanting table, only at a grindstone.
