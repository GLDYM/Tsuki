# Clothing & Armor

## Clothing

- Straw Hat<item id="tsuki:straw_hat"/>: Available from the Japanese Trader; can also be sold to the Japanese Trader.
- Haori<item id="tsuki:haori_light_blue"/>: Available from the Japanese Trader. The black haori can be sold to the Japanese Trader.
- Kimono<item id="tsuki:kimono_white"/>, Yukata<item id="tsuki:yukata_red"/>: Available from the Japanese Trader; the white kimono can be sold to the Japanese Trader.
